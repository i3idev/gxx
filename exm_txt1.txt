The old house stood at the end of a narrow road.
Nobody had lived there for many years. The windows were covered with dust, and the wooden door was slowly falling apart.
One evening, I decided to explore the house.
I pushed the door open and stepped inside. The room was dark and completely silent.
I walked slowly through the hallway. Every step made the old wooden floor creak beneath my feet.
Then I heard a strange noise coming from upstairs.
I stopped.
For a few seconds, I stood completely still and listened.
The noise came again.
This time, it sounded like someone was walking across the floor.
