The old house stood at the end of a narrow road. Nobody had lived there for many years. The windows were covered with dust, and the wooden door was slowly falling apart.

One evening, I decided to explore the house. I pushed the door open and stepped inside. The room was dark and completely silent. I walked slowly through the hallway. Every step made the old wooden floor creak beneath my feet.

Then I heard a strange noise coming from upstairs. I stopped. For a few seconds, I stood completely still and listened. The noise came again. This time, it sounded like someone was walking across the floor.

My heart started to race. Should I run away or go upstairs? I took a deep breath and began to climb the stairs. Each step creaked louder than the last.

At the top, I found a bedroom. The door was slightly open. I pushed it wider and looked inside. The room was empty except for an old rocking chair in the corner. It was moving slowly, back and forth, as if someone had just been sitting there.

But there was nobody there.
